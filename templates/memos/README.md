# Memos

A lightweight, privacy-first place for notes: write in Markdown, tag, search,
and share individual memos.

## What gets deployed

| Service | Image | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Memos** | `neosmemo/memos:0.31.0` | Public. 1 GiB volume for notes (SQLite) and attachments |

## After deploy

Open the URL and **sign up**. The first account becomes the admin. Then go to
**Settings → System** and disable open sign-ups unless you want them.
